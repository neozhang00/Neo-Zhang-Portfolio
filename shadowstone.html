What is Shadowstone

Shadowstone is a co-op tactical roguelite for 1–4 players that explores the darker side of the Sunderfolk universe. Master positioning, build hero synergies, and chain your skills to survive the horrors of the Sunderlands.

COMBAT DESIGN

Design Thesis

Enemies are an ecosystem, not a lineup of isolated threats. Every kit is built to feed into the others and the biome mechanics, so any combination the game spawns becomes a fresh, readable tactical puzzle every round and a new player story every run.

Combat System Overview

Every enemy belongs to one of three classes, each with its own design ruleset that gives it a clear role and goal.

  • Minions are fragile but great at setting the board for the enemies that follow.
  • Mobs carry each biome's defining mechanics and synergize most with it.
  • Bruisers hit hardest and cash in on the setup, but move predictably.

While enemies' strength lies in their synergy, every threat comes with built-in counterplay, giving players a way out of any sticky situation.

Ecosystem in Action

The Abandoned Laboratory's theme is Displacement. Every enemy kit plays off the biome's web-creation mechanic and off each other:

  • Sludges pull players toward other enemies.
  • Sentinels throw players next to enemies or across webs.
  • Spiders leap over webs to reach players others can't.

Players can turn the webs against enemies, using them as barriers that block attacks or keep enemies from completing parts of their kits.

Selling the Hit

Driving combat and enemy design by defining what each mechanic must communicate visually, such as a tongue or hook to telegraph a pull. From there, I trust Engineering, Animation, 3D Art, and VFX, the experts in their craft, to decide how to bring it to life.

Through iteration, I give feedback on timing, silhouettes, camera shake, and effects, working with the team until every action reads clearly and lands with weight.

SYSTEMS DESIGN

Design Thesis

Beyond the core systems, smaller systems are designed to evolve a run without disrupting it. They add mid-run challenge without changing familiar content, keep new content worth engaging with, and place players in control of their run.

Example: Agnostic System

The Agnostic System adds mid- to late-game content to Shadowstone. It shakes up runs with Unstable Rooms and the Sentry, a persistent enemy that scales with each act and follows players between rooms.

The system is optional and keeps the default game loop intact, while rewarding players who take the risk. It also sets up future content and story, starting with Act 4.
