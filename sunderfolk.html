What is Sunderfolk

Sunderfolk is a couch co-op tactical RPG inspired by game nights. Up to four players join using their phones as controllers, with mouse and keyboard support also available, and only one copy of the game is needed for the whole group.

Summarized Contribution

  • Led design of 10 levels/missions from paper concept and blockout through ship, anchoring each mission on a distinct feature (puzzle, combat, exploration) that rewards co-op strategy.
  • Owned 10 characters from concept through implementation and ship (1 playable hero, 4 bosses, 5 enemies), turning each character's fantasy into a distinct ability kit, passive, and AI behavior.
  • Co-led creative direction and implementation of 30+ cinematics, streamlining handoffs between Narrative, Art, and Animation to hit tight deadlines without compromising story quality.
  • Balanced the hero against the full enemy roster, tuning power, counterplay, and difficulty so co-op encounters stayed fair, readable, and engaging.
  • Designed for first-time tabletop players, shaping mission flow, pacing, and narrative for accessibility while preserving depth for repeat play.
  • Ran 3+ internal playtests weekly, turning feedback and edge cases into concrete design changes.

COMBAT DESIGN

Hero
VANGUARD

Born for battle, the Vanguard pushes deep into enemy lines where their abilities burn brightest. Small yet mighty, they stand as a fierce contender to the Berserker.

For each Grit, the Vanguard takes 1 less damage when attacked and deals 2 damage to the attacker, reinforcing their role as both a frontliner and a DPS.

Into the Fray

As the first spell available to the player, Into the Fray teaches the Vanguard's unique keyword and the Grit mechanic.

Bladefall

One of the 3 early spells, Bladefall reinforces the Vanguard's role as frontliner by granting extra damage for each adjacent enemy.

DESIGN PROCESS

Ideation

Starting from the product goals for a new playable hero, brainstormed the hero's class fantasy, core mechanics, passive ability, and skill kit.

Passive Ability

Brainstorm passive ability options based on the core mechanic, then collaborate with the lead designer and game director to finalize the design.

Spell Cards

Designed 10 distinct ability cards to support flexible deck building:

  • Starter cards that teach the Vanguard's core mechanics and passive ability.
  • Situational cards that give players unique answers to different scenarios and encounters.
  • An Ultimate card that captures the Vanguard's class identity and drives team synergy.

Implementation, Playtest, and Iteration

Took the hero from concept to a playable prototype, building it in-engine with existing assets as temporary visuals so the team could play it in the three rapid playtests every week.

Owned iteration end to end: turned feedback from each playtest into design changes, solved technical edge cases, and fixed bugs to keep the hero stable and improving throughout development.

Boss Monster
CHAMPION

This charismatic muscle-bound Champion has big plans for the Hero. Trained in the art of the Muscle, they present a unique challenge that forces players to approach the fight differently from their usual high-damage strategies.

With damage capped at 1, raw output won't win this fight — players must turn the Champion's own abilities and the environment against them.

Bombs Away

A ranged AoE whose damage scales with each minion, keeping crowd control crucial.

Spread out to minimize damage. The blast also leaves objects players can use against the Champion.

Galvanize

Past a certain threshold, the Champion summons additional minions. The result is a fun interplay between the boss's abilities, the crowd, and the narrative.

CINEMATIC & CUTSCENE DESIGN

Cinematic Production

In Sunderfolk, tight budgets and a demanding schedule shaped how every cinematic was produced. The majority are built by repurposing existing gameplay animation and finding creative ways to tell a compelling story — reserving costly, custom one-off animation only for the key narrative moments.

Low Budget

100% Gameplay Assets

Medium Budget

75% Gameplay Assets and 25% Custom Animation

High Budget

100% Custom Animation

Storyboard

After identifying the key narrative beats of each cinematic cutscene, I head into the game space to scout potential locations, then draw storyboards — complete with notes and iterations — before executing the full development of the cinematic.

DESIGN PROCESS

Pre-Production & Planning

Analyze the encounter's narrative and gameplay elements to pinpoint the key story beats, then develop concepts that raise the stakes and give players a glimpse of the challenges ahead.

Research & Storyboarding

Survey the assets already available — gameplay animation, environment set dressing, and VFX — and shape them into a narrative pitch that supports the established key story beats. Design the storyboard to carry players through a smooth, natural transition from gameplay into the cutscene and back to gameplay.

Kitbashing & First Draft

Working from the storyboard, build a first draft — or prototype — directly in the engine using only existing gameplay animation, and lock in a shot list that keeps the camera clear of any unpolished areas of the environment.

Author in-engine animation to move characters naturally through the game space, then collaborate closely with the Animation, 3D, and VFX teams to continuously refine and polish the cinematic.

Final Polish

Through iteration and polish alongside the Animation, 3D, and VFX teams, the result is a low-budget cinematic built entirely from in-game assets — the same animation, VFX, and models players already see in the game.

Clever camera angles, deliberate character blocking, and careful pre-planning let the team deliver a low-cost cinematic that still lands its narrative beat — freeing up hours for the higher-priority polish elsewhere in the game.
